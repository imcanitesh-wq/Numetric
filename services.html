Capital Gains & Crypto

CGT, crypto & capital gains sorted

Capital gain tax is a tax that is levied on the profit earned from the sale of a capital asset, such as stocks, real estate, or other types of investments.

The ATO treats cryptocurrency as property. Capital gains tax applies when you dispose of crypto. We specialise in crypto tax calculations including DeFi, NFTs, staking rewards and all exchange transactions.

Business Activity Statement

BAS & GST handled for you

If you are running a business and registered for GST, you need to lodge a Business Activity Statement (BAS). Your Business Activity Statement will help you report Goods and Services Tax (GST), Pay as You Go Instalment (PAYGI), PAYG Withholding, Fuel Tax Credit, & Other Taxes.

If your business income is more than $75,000 a year you need to report your GST every quarter or monthly. We make this simple and stress-free.

Company & Trust

Complete entity compliance & reporting

A Trust Return, also known as a Trust Tax Return, is a document that is filed with the tax authority to report the income and deductions of a trust.

A Company Tax Return is a document that is filed with the tax authority by a company to report its income, deductions, and tax liability for a particular tax year. We handle all entity types with full financial reporting.
